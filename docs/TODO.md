16. Director (Cosserat / Reissner–Mindlin) shell: let the normal drift and buckle. Today the edge store's frame is borrowed from the geometry (`t_e`, `N x t_e`, `N` the mean face normal), so a store-only tilt is incompatible (Codazzi) and only shows through a position buckle. Give the normal its own DOF instead: `shell_frame_block` (one quaternion per vertex, already used by `knotted_surface`) as a third block in the monge demo, its blue axis the director `d_v`. Rows: transverse shear `d_v . (x_j - x_i) ~ 0` at `w_shear` (infinite: today's Kirchhoff behaviour; finite: normals drift off the surface); bending from directors, a Rodrigues coupling `d_j - d_i ~ -S_e (x_j - x_i)` between directors, store and positions (linear with frames frozen per step, so variational) that replaces or blends with `weingarten_gn`; director regularity (Dirichlet on `d`, or `shell_frame_block`'s frame-transport rows) against zero-energy modes. What it buys: a soft tilt (a shape-space tilt rotates directors first and the shear rows pull the positions into the buckle with a lag, instead of the edit being averaged away), director buckling at its own scale (a second, finer buckling channel; visible folds when the director shades or offsets), and `w_shear` as the physical knob between shape edits and position shape. Risks: shear locking at high `w_shear` on coarse meshes (the Mindlin problem), director modes with weak regularity. Coupling form: ARAP with the rotations promoted to DOFs, `E = sum w |(x_j - x_i) - Q_v dbar_ij|^2` jointly over positions and frames plus a smoothness row on neighbouring `Q` (smooth-rotation-enhanced ARAP), so the frame tracks the one-ring polar `R_v` and the mismatch `R_v^T Q_v` splits into transverse shear (the normal axis, Reissner–Mindlin) and drilling (rotation about the normal, given a small stiffness as shells usually do; the in-plane spin drive lives there). `Q_v`'s tangent axes replace the tracked world tangents as the store's chart: the solve transports them with the material (replacing `transport_tangents`), the director regularity row replaces the Dirichlet bake, and the spin-2 store turns with the drill, so a drilling spin turns the curvature axes against the material and the Rodrigues coupling carries that incompatibility into a position buckle. Spin drives: tilting `d` in a non-integrable pattern (`d . curl d != 0`, no surface has those normals) forces buckling; spinning the frame about `d` turns the store's axes. Prototype: tilt and spin drives on the frames only, sweep `w_shear` and the drill stiffness, measure whether and how fast the positions follow.
